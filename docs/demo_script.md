# LEADWISE Demo Script

1. Home — explain the business problem and deterministic/AI architecture.
2. Executive Dashboard — show lead mix and score ranking.
3. Lead Ranking — show ranked shortlist and CRM export.
4. Lead Analysis — select a lead and explain score drivers.
5. Prioritization Simulator — compare Engagement First vs Intent First.
6. What-If Analysis — change weights and show ranking changes.
7. AI Sales Assistant — ask: “Why is this lead prioritized where it is, and what should the sales team consider next?”
8. Data Validation — run the invalid test dataset and show detected errors.
9. Close with human-in-the-loop, limitations and future CRM integration.
